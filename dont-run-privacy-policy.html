Privacy Policy for Dont Run

Effective Date: August 1, 2026

Davik ("we," "our," or "us") operates the mobile application Dont Run (the "Game") on the Android platform (Package: com.davik.dontrun). This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Game and the choices you have associated with that data.

By downloading, installing, or using Dont Run, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personal data directly from you (such as your name, email address, or phone number) during gameplay. However, the Game integrates third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs) that may collect certain information automatically to ensure smooth gameplay, analytics, monetization, and secure transaction processing.

Types of Data Collected Automatically:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), and mobile network information.
  • Usage Data: In-game progress, chapter & wave completion stats, combat choices, equipment items used, level-up upgrade choices, session duration, and app crash logs.
  • Transaction Data: Internal purchase status tokens when you perform in-app purchases (e.g., unlocking the Mage class or additional inventory slots).

2. Third-Party Services and Data Shared

The Game uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. Below is the list of third-party providers we use, what they are used for, and links to their privacy policies:

A. Google Firebase (Analytics & Crashlytics)

Purpose: We use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to monitor game stability, track crash reports, analyze player retention, understand progression pacing, and improve overall game performance.

Data collected: Device state, system crash logs, session duration, and custom in-game event milestones.

Privacy Policy: Google Privacy & Terms

B. Google AdMob (Advertising)

Purpose: To display non-intrusive advertisements (such as interstitial ads upon retrying and rewarded video ads for player revives) within the game.

Data collected: Advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID), IP addresses, device parameters, and ad interaction metrics.

Privacy Policy: Google AdMob Privacy Policy

C. Google Play Billing SDK (In-App Purchases - IAP)

Purpose: To facilitate secure financial transactions for virtual in-game items (such as unlocking the Mage playable class or extra inventory slots).

Data processed: Google processes all payment instruments directly. We do not store or have access to your credit card details or financial information; we only receive purchase validation tokens from Google Play.

Privacy Policy: Google Play Services Privacy Policy

3. Children's Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions within the EU). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under these age limits. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we will be able to take the necessary actions.

4. Data Security

We value your trust in providing us with your information. Please remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security. However, by relying on major infrastructure like Google Firebase and Google Play Billing, your data benefits from industry-standard security protocols.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted.
